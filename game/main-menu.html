Cyber Pro: Tallinn Defenders

Welcome, Junior Officer

It’s a quiet morning in Tallinn. The old city’s rooftops shimmer with data signals, and inside the new Cyber Department HQ, a fresh recruit steps through the glass doors — you.

You’ve always been fascinated by how the internet connects everything: schools, shops, and even traffic lights. But lately, something’s been wrong. Reports of strange emails, hacked accounts, and disappearing homework files have flooded in. The Chief says someone—or something—is testing the city’s defences.

That’s why you’re here. You passed the Cyber Aptitude Test, proving you can think like both a detective and a coder. Now, as a Junior Officer, you’ll help the department track down digital criminals and protect Tallinn’s networks from cyber threats.
